# Questions for the one-time lawyer review

Bring this folder, a few example company and product pages, and one example of
each recall link type.

1. **ODbL share-alike.** Is our use of Open Food Facts data a "Publicly Used
   Derivative Database"? Is the export in `odbl-share-alike.md` (OFF-derived
   tables only) enough, or does more of the database have to be shared? Does the
   attribution wording on the site satisfy ODbL 4.2/4.3?
2. **No human review.** The original plan made a human review of AI output the
   key legal safeguard. The current pipeline uses automated AI review plus
   user-reported errors, with no human check before publishing (categories,
   company vetting, some recall links). Is that acceptable if disclosed and
   paired with a fast corrections process? Which items, if any, should require
   human review before going live (recall links and forced-labor findings are
   the obvious candidates)?
3. **Recall wording.** Review the evidence notes in `src/lib/recalls.ts` and the
   supply-chain/retailer banners on company pages. Is linking a supplier's or
   co-packer's recall to a brand owner's page, with the explanation shown,
   safe?
4. **Forced-labor (CBP) findings.** Any special care for showing these on a
   company page?
5. **Non-GMO Project data.** Can the Verified product spreadsheet obtained via
   their request form be republished, and how may their program names be used?
6. **Trademarks.** Nominative use of brand names; confirm no logos.
7. **Entity and venue.** Best state for the LLC given anti-SLAPP protection;
   governing-law clause in the terms.
8. **Terms, privacy, disclaimers.** Review drafts once written.
9. **AI disclosure.** Any state law that requires labelling AI-generated or
   AI-decided content of this kind?
10. **Insurance.** When media/E&O insurance becomes worth it.
