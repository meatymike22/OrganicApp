NMSDC (National Minority Supplier Development Council) — for MBE certification
https://nmsdc.org
They have a "Certified Supplier Search" / directory tool where you can look up whether a company holds active NMSDC MBE certification.

WBENC (Women's Business Enterprise National Council) — for WBE certification
https://www.wbenc.org
Similarly has a certified-business search tool (often called "WBENCLink" or a public directory) to verify active WBE-certified companies.

SBA (Small Business Administration) — for 8(a) certification and veteran-owned status
https://www.sba.gov

8(a) Business Development Program status: searchable via SBA's certification database
Veteran-owned: SBA's Veteran Small Business Certification (VetCert) program, https://veterans.certify.sba.gov, has its own public search

State-level DBE (Disadvantaged Business Enterprise) certification
This one's trickier — it's not centralized. Each state (and sometimes each transportation authority) runs its own DBE directory, typically tied to their Department of Transportation. You'd search "[state] DBE directory" for whichever state a company is registered in.

Investor filing:
https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/
